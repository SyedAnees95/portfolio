Account Deletion Request

This page explains how users of Moon Jee Hair Saloon can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you want to delete your account, please send an account deletion request using the email address associated with your account.

Email your request to: aneesulhussnain512@gmail.com

Please include your registered email address and mention that you want your Moon Jee Hair Saloon account deleted.

Data That Will Be Deleted

After verifying your request, we will delete the following data:

  • User account information (name and email address)
  • Profile information
  • Appointment and booking records associated with your account

Data Retention

Some information may be retained if required for legal, security, or business purposes. Any retained data will only be kept for the required period and then permanently deleted.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 days after verification.
